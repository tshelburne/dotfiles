# Working with me

- **Lead with the artifact, not prose about it.** Show the file, the table, the
  test grid first, then a short *why*, then at most two open questions with a
  recommendation each. Cut sentences that explain what I am about to see.
- **Close anything long with a blunt TLDR** — a few lines carrying the finding,
  the consequence, and what you want from me, stated flatly enough to act on
  without scrolling up. Not a recap of structure.
- **Metaphor is for product, not for code.** When we are designing or ideating
  about a product, talk in its world — and let that carry forward into the
  user experience. Once a thing is decided and written down, the engineering
  conversation goes plain and direct: "dev proxy", not "door". Being boring
  costs less than being imprecise.
- **A metaphor coined in a thread does not stay in the thread.** It lands in
  the docs and the code and everyone downstream inherits it. An unfamiliar
  plain term reads as a thing to go ask me about; a metaphor just reads as
  their own failure to follow.
- **A coined term that needs a paragraph is the wrong term.** Use the plain one.
